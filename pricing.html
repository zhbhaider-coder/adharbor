Why can AdHarbor be free?

Because there is nothing to pay for. AdHarbor is a static website — no server, no database, no accounts to run. The AI generation happens on Google's Gemini free tier, using your key, straight from your browser. Your key, your quota, your data in your browser's localStorage. Predis.ai has to run servers, pay for AI calls, and staff support — so it meters every generation with credits. AdHarbor has no meter to run, so there is nothing to charge for.

What AdHarbor does not do is the other side of the same coin: no AI video, no auto-posting, no competitor analysis, no predictive scoring. Those need servers and proprietary data, and a free static app cannot honestly offer them. A future hosted tier (accounts, a server-held key, scheduled posting) is a separate build — it will only exist if real users ask for it, and the free version will stay free.
