FAQ

Is AdHarbor really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no account and no card. You bring your own free Gemini API key (free from Google AI Studio, no card required) and AdHarbor generates as much as you want — no credits, no monthly meter.

How is AdHarbor different from Predis.ai?

Predis.ai charges $32 to $249 a month, rations every generation with credits, and requires a card for its 3-day trial. AdHarbor does the same core jobs — ad copy, social posts, captions, hashtags, ad creatives — free and unlimited, and adds email copy and product descriptions in the same brief, which Predis does not cover.

Does AdHarbor post to my social accounts automatically?

No — auto-posting needs a server and account connections, and AdHarbor is a static app with no server. Instead you plan in the built-in content calendar and export captions as CSV, the calendar as an ICS file, and creatives as PNGs, then post from your phone or scheduler in seconds.

Can AdHarbor make AI videos?

No. AI video and AI image generation need paid GPU services — Gemini image models are paid-only and have no free tier. AdHarbor generates text with your free Gemini key and renders ad creatives on a canvas from templates, your photos and free stock photos, which is genuinely free.

Is my API key safe?

Your key is stored only in your browser's localStorage and is sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API. It is never stored on any server — AdHarbor has no server at all — and never leaves your device except to call Google.

What does AdHarbor not do?

It does not do AI video or avatars, auto-posting, competitor analysis, or predictive performance scoring. Those need servers and proprietary data. AdHarbor is honest about that and wins on creation: one brief, a full campaign kit, unlimited.
