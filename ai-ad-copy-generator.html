Write ad copy that converts: 5 rules the generator follows

  1. One idea per ad. A variant that tries to say everything says nothing.
  2. Lead with the benefit, not the feature. "40-hour burn" is a feature; "an evening that smells like calm" is a benefit.
  3. Match the platform's length. Google headlines cap at 30 characters; TikTok hooks die after one line.
  4. Variants must be genuinely different. Three angles — price, story, proof — not three rewordings.
  5. Ban your crutch words. Put "amazing", "revolutionary" and "best in the world" in your banned list and watch the copy get specific.
