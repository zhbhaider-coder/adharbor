# BACKLINKS.md — AdHarbor

Free all-in-one AI marketing suite: ad copy, social posts, captions, hashtags,
email and ad creatives from one brief. Unlimited, no credits, no card, no
account. Live: https://adharbor.pages.dev/

## 1. What the site is (one line)

AdHarbor — a free, unlimited, all-in-one AI marketing suite (ad copy, captions,
hashtags, email, ad creatives, content calendar) that runs in the browser with
your own free Gemini key.

## 2. Why anyone would link to it

- It is free and unlimited where the category leader (Predis.ai) charges
  $32–$249/month and rations everything with credits.
- It does email copy and product descriptions in the same brief — Predis.ai
  does not cover either.
- 20+ canvas ad-creative templates with exact-size PNG export, a persistent
  Brand Voice, and a content calendar with CSV/ICS/PNG export — all in one
  free static app, no account.
- Honest about its limits: no AI video, no auto-posting, no fake AI images.
