Predis.ai is the closest all-in-one AI marketing tool — posts, captions, hashtags, creatives, scheduling. It is also $32 to $249 a month, credit-metered, and card-required. AdHarbor does the same core jobs free, unlimited, with no account.

Every plan starts with a 3-day free trial that requires a card — and is charged when day 3 ends. Add-ons: extra credits $29/month, extra brand $5/month, extra channels $25–$99/month. Unused credits expire monthly, and video generations burn them several times faster than images. Failed video generations still consume credits, according to user reviews on Trustpilot and G2.

What users complain about

Predis.ai scores well — G2 4.7/5, Trustpilot 4.5/5 — but the complaints cluster in the same places: credits drain faster than advertised, bulk generation from bare prompts yields near-duplicate posts, video voiceovers sound mechanical, analytics are thin, and the trial has charged users before it ended, with a strict no-refund policy. The most common surprise is the annual charge taken in one go — around $500 on Rise — which users did not expect.

What AdHarbor does that Predis doesn't

One brief, everything at once. Predis makes you generate posts, captions and creatives in separate flows. AdHarbor takes one brief and returns the whole kit — ad copy for four platforms, captions, hashtags, an email and a product description — in a single generation.

Email and product descriptions. Predis does not cover email marketing or product-page copy. AdHarbor writes both from the same brief, because a campaign is more than a social post.

A real creative studio. 20+ editable templates, your brand kit, your own photos or free stock photos, and PNG export at the exact sizes — 1080×1080, 1080×1920, 1200×628, 1080×1350. No credits per download; AdCreative-style tools charge about $3.90 per creative on their entry plan.

What AdHarbor cannot do: AI video, auto-posting and competitor analysis. Those need servers, account connections and proprietary data. If you need true auto-posting, Predis Rise or a scheduler like Buffer is the honest choice. If you need unlimited creation without a meter, AdHarbor is the free one.
