Content Calendar

Plan posts from your saved kits, then export everything at once. AdHarbor does not auto-post — that needs a server and account connections, and this app has neither. So it gives you the next best thing, honestly: a plan you can export and post from your phone in seconds.

How posting works here: pick a day, attach a kit (or write a caption), and export. Captions go out as a CSV you can open anywhere, the plan as an ICS file you can import into Google Calendar, and creatives as PNG files. Then post from your phone, or paste the CSV into Buffer or Meta Business Suite. Predis.ai caps auto-posting at ~2–3 posts a day and has no auto-posting at all on its $32/month Core plan — AdHarbor's export has no cap.
