How it is used

Every generation in the Campaign Studio gets your voice appended to the prompt: tone, audience, banned words, winning hooks, brand name and tagline. The model is told to write in that voice and never use the banned words.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your Brand Voice is stored in this browser's localStorage — clear your browser data and it is gone, so keep a copy of your hooks somewhere safe if they matter.

Tips that actually help

· Banned words beat tone. One banned word ("cheap", "amazing") does more for your voice than a paragraph of tone description.

· Paste real winners. Two or three hooks that actually got clicks teach the model more than any adjective.

· Name the audience, not the demographic. "Busy mums who buy gifts at 11pm" beats "women 25–45".

· Regenerate per section. If one block of a kit sounds off, hit its Regenerate button — the rest of the kit stays.
